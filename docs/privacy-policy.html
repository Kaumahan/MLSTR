Privacy Policy – Ace Counter Pick

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Ace Counter Pick ("the app") is an unofficial fan tool that shows hero counter picks, builds and first-pick suggestions in a floating bubble. This policy explains what the app does with data.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It does not read your screen, your game, your contacts, your location or any files.

Network requests

To show up-to-date hero information, the app downloads public game statistics from the Rone Arena API (arena.rone.dev) and hero/item images through the wsrv.nl image service. Like any website you visit, those services receive your device's IP address and basic request information in order to answer the request. The app does not send them any personal information. Their own privacy practices apply to those requests.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the downloaded public hero data and images on your device so it opens quickly and works offline. This cache contains no personal information and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Permissions

  • Display over other apps: used only to show the floating bubble and panel on top of your game. You turn it on yourself in Android settings and can turn it off at any time.
  • Internet: used to download public hero statistics and images.
  • Foreground service / notifications: keeps the bubble running while you play and shows a notification with a button to close it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
